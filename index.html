    Math with LaTeX

    Inline math

    Wrap with $ ... $ or \( ... \). Escaped form is safest:

    Energy is \( E = mc^2 \).
Also works: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
    Display (centered) math

    Wrap with $$ ... $$ or \[ ... \], alone on its own lines:

    \[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
\]
    • Inline $ ... $ must sit on one line and cannot have a space right after $ (use \( \) if unsure).
    • Math inside code fences or `inline code` is never rendered as math — safe for literal $ signs.
    • Common commands work: \frac{a}{b}, \sqrt{x}, \alpha, \sum_{i=1}^{n}, superscripts x^2, subscripts x_i.
